# Changelog

What changed in each Uzero release, written for people who use it. uzero.app/releases shows the same list.

Add a section for the next version before you run `scripts/release.sh`. The release workflow puts that section at the top of the GitHub release.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-18)

- Reorder in Google now lists signed-out accounts too. Remove any account before you start.
- Add an account that is not signed in yet. You sign in to it during the reorder, in the spot you picked.
- Links to uzero.app, Privacy and Terms at the bottom of the popup, side panel and Settings.
- Your numbers: Settings shows what Uzero has done for you. The counts stay on your device.
- Shorter text across the app, and small fixes.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-18)

- Rate Uzero from Settings, or after a reorder that worked.
- Dropdowns in Settings give their arrow room.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- First release, on Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
- Put your signed-in Google accounts in your order.
- Pin opens Google links in the account you pick, per Google service.
- Reorder in Google walks you through signing out and back in so `/u/0` is the account you want.
