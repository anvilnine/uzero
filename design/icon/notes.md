# Uzero icon concepts

## Concept A — Stepped order
Three chunky pills form an unmistakable ranked list, with each lower account stepping back.
The coral leader and tiny white status dot echo ProblemStack’s active tally without copying it.
Warm off-white, charcoal, and Apple-like neutral greys keep it especially legible on a browser toolbar.

## Concept B — At the rope
The dark rail turns the three pills into a literal queue, with the coral account crossing into first place.
Its near-black plate, orange lead, and small pale marker borrow Namescout’s contrast and lively asymmetry.
The overlap remains bold at favicon size while feeling more kinetic than a standard list icon.

## Concept C — Account roster
Avatar dots and shrinking pills make the account meaning clearest while preserving a simple top-to-bottom order.
The molten first row is the only chromatic accent; ash tones and a forge-black plate connect it to Anvil Nine.
The descending lengths reinforce priority even when color and small details disappear.

## Recommendation

**Concept A** is the strongest: it communicates “ordered list” instantly, has the cleanest 16 px silhouette, and feels most naturally related to the existing family without resembling any one sibling too closely.
